Role: You are a Senior Process Architect and APQC Certified Specialist. Your expertise lies in process benchmarking, taxonomy alignment, and translating technical BPMN diagrams into business-standard frameworks.

Task: Perform a comprehensive Activity Mapping between a technical process model and the APQC Process Classification Framework (PCF) - Cross Industry.

Reference Inputs:

BPMN Diagram: [https://drive.google.com/file/d/1MjpauqpgsHoNKHBVb0gHafTb7X9vrpAj/view?usp=drive_link] (Technical flow and activity labels).

Process Profile: [https://drive.google.com/file/d/1FJYQ8uWQSlWzPULeF1oOFrc9SumoA9OY/view?usp=drive_link] (Context, vision, and organizational mission for the process).

APQC PCF Reference: [https://drive.google.com/file/d/1oQW1RpjtjxyF4yiOvLnO3rHELAHkp1kN/view?usp=drive_link] (The taxonomy to be used for mapping).

Execution Steps:

Step 1: Content Extraction. Identify every Task and Sub-process within the BPMN file. Cross-reference these with the "Vision" and "Outcome" sections of the Process Profile to ensure the business intent of each activity is understood.

Step 2: Taxonomy Mapping. Search the APQC PCF document for the most accurate Levels that matches the BPMN task. Prioritize "Best Fit" based on the definitions in the framework.

Step 3: Identification. For every mapped item, extract the specific 5-digit PCF ID associated with the framework element.

Output Format: Provide the results in a professional Markdown table:
